Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

This policy explains how GuardLink handles information.

01

Who We Are

GuardLink is a privately operated recruiting-support platform owned by Pico Duarte Holdings LLC and operated by GuardLink Technologies LLC. This policy applies to guardlink.site, GuardLink accounts, forms, and related communications.
02

Information We Collect

We collect information you provide, including contact details, recruiter or candidate profile information, lead and workflow data, and messages. We may also collect basic device, browser, security, and usage data needed to operate and protect the platform.
03

How We Use Information

We use information to provide the service, route inquiries, support recruiting workflows, authenticate accounts, prevent misuse, improve features, and communicate about service or security updates.
04

Recruiting and Government Information

Do not submit classified information, controlled unclassified information, medical records, Social Security numbers, financial account details, or other information that is not approved for the platform. Users remain responsible for applicable agency and command data-handling rules.
05

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We may share information with service providers that help operate GuardLink, authorized organizational administrators, when you direct us, or when law requires it.
06

Cookies and Analytics

GuardLink may use essential browser storage, security tools, and measurement services to keep users signed in, remember preferences, detect abuse, and understand site performance.
07

Retention and Security

We retain information only as long as reasonably needed for the service, legal obligations, security, and disputes. We use reasonable safeguards, but no online system can guarantee absolute security.
08

Your Choices

You may request access, correction, or deletion of personal information, subject to legal and operational retention requirements. Organization-managed accounts may also be controlled by an authorized administrator.
09

Children

GuardLink is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
10

Changes and Contact

We may update this policy by posting a revised version here. Contact support@guardlink.site with privacy questions or requests.
