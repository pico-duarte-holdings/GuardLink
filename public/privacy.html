Privacy Policy for GuardLink

Last Updated: March 24, 2026

1. Introduction

GuardLink Technologies LLC ("we", "us", or "our") operates the GuardLink platform (guardlink.site). We are committed to protecting the privacy and security of the data entrusted to us by recruiting personnel and state commands. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information to provide and improve our Service:

  • Account Information: When you register, we collect your name, email address, phone number, and professional details (e.g., rank, recruiting station, state command).
  • Operational Data: We securely store the data you input into the platform regarding recruiting leads, workflows, and accession tracking.
  • Usage Data: We automatically collect standard diagnostic data, including your IP address, browser type, and how you interact with the platform, to monitor system performance and security.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected data strictly to:

  • Provide, maintain, and support the GuardLink platform.
  • Authenticate your login credentials and secure your account.
  • Analyze usage trends to improve features and user experience.
  • Communicate with you regarding account updates, technical notices, and security alerts.

4. Data Sharing and Disclosure

We do not sell your personal data or operational data to third parties. We will only share your information under the following circumstances:

  • Service Providers: With trusted third-party vendors (such as secure cloud hosting providers) who assist us in operating the platform, under strict confidentiality agreements.
  • Legal Compliance: If required by law, subpoena, or request from government or military authorities.
  • Command Oversight: If your account is provisioned by or linked to a state-level command, authorized leadership may have access to organizational usage and workflow data.

5. Data Security

GuardLink Technologies LLC implements industry-standard security measures, including encryption and secure server hosting, to protect your data from unauthorized access, alteration, or destruction. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. Users are responsible for ensuring their use of the platform aligns with applicable Department of Defense (DoD) or state-level data handling regulations.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify users of any material changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: support@guardlink.site.
