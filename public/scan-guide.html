SCAN YOUR DOCS

Clean scans. Clear PDFs. Straight to Google Drive. Follow this guide so your paperwork looks sharp.

// PRO TIPS //

White Paper Background

Place a blank white sheet under your document for clean edges and minimal shadows.

Good Lighting

Use natural daylight when possible. Avoid overhead lights that cast shadows.

Flat & Straight

Lay your document completely flat on a hard surface. Smooth out wrinkles and corners.

Steady & Overhead

Hold your phone directly above the document. Shoot straight down, not at an angle.

// GOOD VS BAD //
CLEAN SCAN
Good

Sharp edges, even lighting, text readable

BLURRY ANGLE
Bad

Angled shot, shadows, hard to read

// SCANNING STEPS //
1

Open Notes App

Create a new note, tap the camera icon, select "Scan Documents".

2

Position & Auto-Detect

Position your document in the viewfinder. A yellow border auto-detects the edges.

3

Review & Adjust

Tap shutter or let it auto-capture. Review the crop corners. Tap "Keep Scan".

4

Add More Pages

Scan multi-page docs by adding pages. Tap "Save" when done.

5

Export as PDF

Tap the share icon. Select "Save to Files" or "Print". Choose PDF format.

6

Upload to Google Drive

Open Google Drive app, tap +, select "Upload", choose your PDF.

7

Rename & Verify

Rename as LastName_DocType (e.g., Lantigua_SSCard). Verify upload.

1

Open Google Drive

Launch Google Drive app. Tap + to start.

2

Select "Scan"

Tap "Scan". Position your document, let camera auto-detect edges.

3

Capture & Adjust

Tap capture. Adjust corners if needed. Tap checkmark to confirm.

4

Add More Pages

Tap + for additional pages. Tap "Save" when done.

5

Name & Organize

Name as LastName_DocType. Choose folder, tap Save.

6

Verify & Share

Verify scan is clean and readable. Share link with recruiter if requested.

// QUICK REFERENCE //

Do's

  • Use white background paper
  • Shoot in natural light
  • Keep document flat
  • Hold phone directly overhead
  • Verify text is readable
  • Use clear file naming

Don'ts

  • Don't shoot at an angle
  • Don't have shadows on doc
  • Don't crease or fold pages
  • Don't use bad lighting
  • Don't blur when taking photo
  • Don't forget to name files
