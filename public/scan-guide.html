SCAN YOUR DOCS

Clean scans. Clear PDFs. Straight to Google Drive. Follow this guide so your paperwork looks sharp.

// PRO TIPS //

White Paper Background

Place a blank white sheet under your document for clean edges and minimal shadows. This creates contrast that makes text pop.

Good Lighting

Use natural daylight when possible. Avoid overhead lights that cast shadows. Position yourself so light hits the document evenly.

Flat & Straight

Lay your document completely flat on a hard surface. Smooth out wrinkles and corners. A textbook or clipboard works great.

Steady & Overhead

Hold your phone directly above the document, parallel to the surface. Shoot straight down, not at an angle. Keep hands steady.

// GOOD VS BAD //
✓
Good

Flat, centered, even lighting, all text sharp and readable

✗
Bad

Tilted, finger in frame, shadow across text, blurry

// SCANNING STEPS //
1

Open Notes App

Create a new note and tap the camera icon. Select "Scan Documents" from the menu.

2

Position & Auto-Detect

Position your document in the viewfinder. You'll see a yellow border auto-detect the edges. Hold steady and wait for capture.

3

Review & Adjust

Tap shutter or let it auto-capture. Review the crop corners. Tap "Keep Scan" to confirm the capture.

4

Add More Pages

If scanning multi-page documents, tap to add more pages. Once done, tap "Save" to finalize.

5

Export as PDF

Tap the share icon (box with arrow). Select "Save to Files" or "Print". Choose PDF format.

6

Upload to Google Drive

Open Google Drive app. Tap + button. Select "Upload" and choose your PDF file from Files.

7

Rename & Verify

Rename the file as LastName_DocType (e.g., Lantigua_SSCard). Verify upload is complete.

1

Open Google Drive

Launch the Google Drive app. Tap the + button to start a new upload or action.

2

Select "Scan"

From the menu, tap "Scan". Position your document and let the camera auto-detect edges.

3

Capture & Adjust

Tap the capture button. Review the crop and adjust corners if needed. Tap the checkmark to confirm.

4

Add More Pages

Tap + to scan additional pages. When done, tap "Save" to finalize the scan.

5

Name & Organize

Name the file as LastName_DocType. Choose the folder and tap Save.

6

Verify & Share

Verify the scan looks clean and readable. Share the link with your recruiter if requested.

  • Use white background paper

  • Hold phone directly overhead

✗ Don'ts

  • Don't shoot at an angle

  • Don't use bad lighting
  • Don't blur when taking photo
  • Don't forget to name files
